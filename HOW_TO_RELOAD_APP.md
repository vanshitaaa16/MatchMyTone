# 🔄 How to Reload Your Expo App

## Method 1: Using Expo Terminal (Easiest)

1. **Go to the terminal where Expo is running**
   - This is the terminal that shows the QR code and "Metro waiting on..."

2. **Press the letter `r`** (lowercase)
   - Just press `r` once
   - Don't type anything else, just `r`

3. **You'll see:**
   ```
   Reloading apps...
   ```
   - Your app will automatically reload on your phone!

---

## Method 2: Shake Your Phone

1. **On your phone**, open the Expo Go app
2. **Shake your phone** (like you're shaking it)
3. **A menu will appear**
4. **Tap "Reload"** or **"Reload App"**

---

## Method 3: Close and Reopen Expo Go

1. **Close Expo Go app** on your phone (swipe it away)
2. **Open Expo Go again**
3. **Scan the QR code** again (or it might auto-reconnect)
4. **App will reload**

---

## Method 4: Press 'r' in Expo Terminal (Recommended)

**This is the easiest method:**

1. **Click on the Expo terminal window** (where you ran `npx expo start`)
2. **Make sure the terminal is active** (click in it)
3. **Press `r`** (just the letter r, nothing else)
4. **Done!** Your app reloads automatically

---

## ✅ After Reloading:

1. **Wait a few seconds** for the app to reload
2. **Try logging in** - should work now!
3. **The ngrok warning won't appear** in your app

---

## 🎯 Quick Steps:

1. **Click Expo terminal** (where QR code is)
2. **Press `r`**
3. **Wait for reload**
4. **Try login!**

That's it! 🚀
