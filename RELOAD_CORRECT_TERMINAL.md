# ⚠️ Wrong Terminal!

## The Issue:
You're in a **regular Command Prompt** - that's not the right place!

## ✅ What You Need:
You need to press `r` in the **Expo terminal** (where `npx expo start` is running).

---

## 🎯 Find the Right Terminal:

### Look for the Terminal That Shows:
- QR code
- "Metro waiting on exp://..."
- "Scan the QR code above with Expo Go"
- "Press r | reload app"

**That's the Expo terminal!**

---

## ✅ Steps:

1. **Find the Expo terminal window**
   - It's the one where you ran `npx expo start`
   - It shows the QR code and Metro bundler info

2. **Click inside that terminal window**

3. **Press `r`** (just the letter r)

4. **You'll see:** "Reloading apps..."

5. **Your phone app will reload automatically!**

---

## 🔍 Can't Find the Expo Terminal?

### Option 1: Start Expo Again
If you closed it, just run:
```bash
npx expo start
```
Then press `r` in that terminal.

### Option 2: Shake Your Phone
1. Open Expo Go on your phone
2. **Shake your phone**
3. Tap **"Reload"**

---

## 📱 Quick Alternative:
**Just shake your phone** with Expo Go open and tap "Reload" - that's the easiest way!

---

## Summary:
- ❌ **NOT** in regular Command Prompt
- ✅ **YES** in Expo terminal (where QR code is shown)
- ✅ **OR** shake your phone and tap "Reload"

Try shaking your phone - that's the easiest! 🚀
