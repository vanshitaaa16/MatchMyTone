# 📱 Reconnect Expo Go App

## The Issue:
"No apps connected" means your phone's Expo Go app is not connected to the Expo server.

## ✅ Quick Fix:

### Option 1: Scan QR Code Again (Easiest)

1. **On your phone**, open **Expo Go app**
2. **Tap "Scan QR code"** (or use the camera)
3. **Scan the QR code** shown in your terminal
4. **App will connect and reload automatically!**

---

### Option 2: Shake Phone and Reload

1. **On your phone**, make sure **Expo Go is open**
2. **Shake your phone**
3. **Tap "Reload"** in the menu
4. **App will reload!**

---

### Option 3: Close and Reopen Expo Go

1. **Close Expo Go** on your phone (swipe it away)
2. **Open Expo Go again**
3. **Scan the QR code** from your terminal
4. **App will connect!**

---

## 🎯 Recommended Steps:

1. **On your phone**, open **Expo Go**
2. **Tap "Scan QR code"** (or the camera icon)
3. **Point camera at the QR code** in your terminal
4. **Wait for it to connect** (you'll see "Connected" in terminal)
5. **App will load automatically!**
6. **Try logging in** - should work now! ✅

---

## ✅ After Reconnecting:

- The app will load on your phone
- You can then try logging in
- The ngrok URL will work through the app
- No more connection errors!

---

## Summary:
Just **scan the QR code again** in Expo Go - that's the easiest way to reconnect! 🚀
